# RESONANCE — the core pitch

*One story, told at three depths. Every claim below survives a skeptic. The wonder is in the true version; nothing unfalsifiable is load-bearing.*

---

## The one sentence

**People gathering in shared rhythm and intention, in spaces that acoustically bind them, enter a real, measurable state of collective synchrony — and we've built the tool to design that state on purpose, fairly, at any scale.**

---

## Why it's real (the part that survives scrutiny)

Human beings synchronize. It's measurable, not mystical:

- Singing or moving together entrains people's breathing and heart rates toward a shared rhythm.
- Audiences hearing the same music show synchronized neural activity across individuals.
- Crowds unconsciously fall into step — enough to sway a bridge.

This is coupled oscillation — the same physics that tunes a bell's partials into a fixed ratio and that engineers design *around* so a tower doesn't shake itself apart. A resonant room (a cathedral, a stone hall, a well-built venue) physically binds a gathering's sound into one sustained field, which is why humans have chased this state in sacred and celebratory spaces for all of recorded history.

**The claim we make is only the measurable one.** What a person feels it *means* — spiritually, personally — is theirs to hold. We honor that dimension; we don't fold it into the claim. That separation is what makes the framework trustworthy.

## What we built

**Tonal** — a platform that lets anyone compose a gathering as an intentional vessel:

- A full crew taxonomy (58 role functions across 9 departments) that scales from a bar night to a festival — *functions to cover, not headcount to hire*.
- Each role carries a real agreement (before / during / after), sourcing guidance (direct hire vs. staffing agency vs. licensed provider), and onboarding — with worker data and payments handled by compliant third parties, never by us.
- A transparent, care-first economic model: **capital and curation stay separate**, backers recoup first from a clear waterfall, hard costs and crew are paid before profit splits.
- A create-event flow, role dashboards, and a curated provider network — with a genuinely free default path so it costs organizers and backers as little as possible.

It's already robust. It carries real information and real intention. The work now is lapidation — polishing the facets — and finding the right people to manifest it.

## The three audiences (same stone, different facet)

**Structure** (venues, hosts, sacred spaces)
*They care about reverence and stewardship.*
Lead with respect for what they've built; make a small, aligned first ask; prove you'll treat the room and the community with care. The science is quiet backbone, never the headline. → *"Your space already does something rare — it binds many voices into one. We want to meet it on its own terms, honor it, and treat it with the care it deserves."*

**Money** (backers, investors)
*They care about whether it's real and returnable.*
Vision stays in the back pocket; lead with the working product, who wants it, and how the model returns capital. Our credibility here is that we've already engineered *their* risk down: recoup-first waterfall, transparent ledger, capital/curation separation. → *"Here's a working platform, a defined market, and a fair, transparent return structure that pays costs and crew before profit."*

**Influence** (artists, connectors, tastemakers)
*They care about authenticity and being early to something true.*
Here the vision *is* the pitch — but the grounded, bully-proofed version. They can smell a manufactured movement; give them the real one. → *"This is the physics of why gathering feels the way it does, built into a tool that makes it fair and repeatable. Be early."*

---

## The rule that keeps it honest

For every new idea that wants into the framework, ask one question:

> **Could I measure this — or do I have to believe it?**

Measurable goes in the framework. Believable-but-not-measurable goes in the art, the ritual, the personal meaning — a real and honored place, just not inside the claims. This filter is what lets skeptics respect it and believers still feel at home.
